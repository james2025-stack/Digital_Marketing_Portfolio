Kahawa Corner

Social media launch campaign for a new specialty coffee shop

Goal
Build awareness and drive foot traffic in the first 60 days
Audience
Students and young professionals, 18 to 35, active on Instagram and TikTok
Budget
KES 15,000 in paid boosts on Reels and Stories
Platforms
Instagram (main), TikTok (reach), WhatsApp Business (orders and loyalty)

Content pillars

  • Behind the scenes: 35%
  • Product shots and reels: 30%
  • Customer-made content: 20%
  • Promotions: 15%

Execution

  1. Week 1: Teaser posts and a 7-day countdown to opening.
  2. Week 2: Opening-day giveaway: tag 2 friends to win a free month of coffee.
  3. Weeks 3 to 8: Three reels a week, a Friday "Barista Picks" series, and a student discount code.

Targets

  • 2,000 followers
  • 5% engagement rate
  • 300 discount-code redemptions

Mtumba Chic

Instagram and TikTok growth plan for an online secondhand fashion store

Goal
Grow from 500 to 5,000 followers and reach 100 sales a month through DMs
Positioning
Sustainable, affordable, one-of-a-kind style
Tools
Canva, CapCut, Meta Business Suite, Linktree

Growth tactics

  • Collaborations with 5 micro-influencers (5K to 20K followers).
  • A referral discount for customers who bring a friend.
  • Posting at peak hours, 7 to 9 PM.
  • Content series: outfit transformations, "Thrift Haul Tuesday", styling tips, customer try-ons.

Urban Gadgets KE

Email marketing and sales funnel for an online electronics accessories store

Goal
Increase repeat purchases and recover abandoned carts
Tools
Mailchimp or Brevo (both have free plans)

The funnel

  1. Awareness: Facebook and Instagram ads, plus SEO blog posts.
  2. Lead capture: 10% off a first order in exchange for an email address.
  3. Nurture: An automated welcome series.
  4. Conversion: Abandoned-cart emails and limited-time offers.
  5. Retention: Review requests after purchase and loyalty rewards.

Welcome sequence

WhenEmail
Instantly"Welcome! Here's your 10% code"
Day 2Best-sellers and customer reviews
Day 4"How to choose the right power bank" (educational)
Day 7"Your code expires tonight" (urgency)

Subject lines to A/B test

  • A: "You left something behind 👀"
  • B: "Your cart is waiting (and so is 10% off)"

Targets

  • Open rate above 35%
  • Click rate above 3%
  • Cart recovery of 10 to 15%

BrightMinds Tutors

Google Ads and SEO lead generation for a private tutoring service

Goal
50 qualified leads a month from parents
Ad budget
KES 500 a day, with negative keywords to cut wasted spend
Campaign type
Search ads with location targeting

SEO plan

  • Keywords: "KCSE revision tutors", "home tutors near me", "math tutor for primary school".
  • On the page: An optimised landing page, blog posts such as "How to Prepare for KCSE in 90 Days", and a Google Business Profile.
  • Off the page: Local directory listings and backlinks from parent groups.

Sample search ad

Top-Rated KCSE Tutors | Book a Free Trial Qualified teachers. Flexible schedules. Boost your child's grades.

Landing page

A clear headline, testimonials, a short enquiry form, and a click-to-WhatsApp button.

Targets

  • Cost per lead below KES 300
  • Conversion rate above 8%
  • Top-3 Google ranking for 5 keywords
